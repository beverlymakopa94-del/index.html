Natural Resources of Papua New Guinea

Papua New Guinea is a country blessed with abundant natural resources that play a vital role in its economy and culture.

The land is rich in minerals such as gold, copper, silver, nickel, and cobalt, which are extracted from large mining operations like Ok Tedi and Porgera.

Vast tropical forests provide valuable hardwoods, including teak and mahogany, though sustainability is an ongoing concern.

The surrounding seas are equally important, offering fisheries that support both employment and food security, with tuna being a major export.

Agriculture is central to rural livelihoods, with coffee, cocoa, copra, and palm oil grown widely and traded internationally.

In addition, Papua New Guinea has significant energy resources, including oil, natural gas, and hydropower potential, with LNG projects expanding rapidly.

Together, these resources form the backbone of the nation’s economy, while also shaping the daily lives of its people.
